See how it works

No turns

Two listeners run at once, one per language. Nobody taps to take a turn and nobody passes the phone around.

Their half faces them

Large captions in their own language, readable at arm’s length, at a size they choose themselves. They can scroll back to re-read a price or a time.

Never shows an unchecked line

If a translation can't be confirmed, Tabletop hides it rather than risk a wrong number, and tells both of you who should say it again.

Private, and offline once set up

Speech recognition and translation run on your device. After a one-time language pack download it works with no connection.

Keep it. Get the minutes.

Keep a conversation as a letter and read it again later. With Tabletop Pro, minutes are written on your device: who asked what, the figures, and what to do next, each linked to the line it came from.

Designed with iPhone Duo in mind. Works on every iPhone.

Open a Duo and the two halves sit naturally on either side of the fold. On any iPhone with iOS 26 or later, the screen splits the same way. Siri and Shortcuts can start and end a conversation; VoiceOver and Dynamic Type are supported on both halves.

Built for the person across from you

Polite in every language

Ready-made requests such as “Please repeat” and “Please speak more slowly” are written by people, in a courteous register, in all 19 languages.

Names kept as written

Add the names and terms that must never be translated, so “Mai” stays Mai and your company stays your company.

Phrases ready before you go

Write an address or a booking ahead of time and show it full screen, upright and as large as it fits. Or have a sentence translated on your device and check what they will read.

Listen, when someone talks to you

One-way captions in your language for a guide, an announcement or someone explaining. Tap Talk Back when you want to answer.

One press to start

Start from Control Center, the Lock Screen or the Action button. Tabletop keeps interpreting if you switch apps or lock the phone, with Pause and End on the Lock Screen.

See it in your hand

App Store screenshots. The other person's half is upside down to you, so it reads the right way up for them.

Private by default

A stranger's words are not yours to send anywhere. Tabletop keeps them on your device.

  • Speech recognition and translation run on your device.
  • Conversations never go to our servers, or anyone else's. If you choose to keep them, they stay on your device and sync through your own iCloud. App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected.
  • Works offline once the language packs are downloaded: in a taxi, at a market, abroad without data.
  • Keeping conversations is off until you turn it on, and the other person is told when it is.

Questions

Is it accurate?

Tabletop uses machine translation, so it can make mistakes. It hides any line it can't confirm and gives both people a polite way to ask for a repeat. For medical, legal or safety-critical conversations, please use a professional interpreter.

Does it work without internet?

Yes, once you have downloaded the speech and translation packs for both languages. Download them over Wi‑Fi before you travel.

Which iPhones does it run on?

Any iPhone with iOS 26 or later. iPhone Duo is the device it was designed around.

What does it cost?

Tabletop is free to download, and every conversation is free, with no limit: Listen, the camera and phrases too, and the other person never meets a paywall. Tabletop Pro is optional: it writes minutes of the conversations you keep (your first three are free), as a yearly subscription with a free trial or a one-time purchase. Prices are shown in the App Store.

Does the other person need the app?

No. Everything happens on your phone. Their half greets them in their language and tells them it is machine translation.

Is anything recorded?

Not by default. If you choose to keep conversations, they are saved on your device and in your own iCloud, and the other person sees that the conversation is being saved.

Does it keep going if I switch apps or lock the phone?

Yes. Once you start a conversation, Tabletop keeps listening and translating until you end it, so you can check a map or a calculator. A Live Activity shows that it is listening, with Pause and End.
