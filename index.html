Questions

Does the other person need the app?

No. Everything happens on your phone. Their half greets them in their language.

What should I do before I travel?

Download your two languages over Wi‑Fi. After that, no connection is needed.

Which iPhones does it run on?

Any iPhone with iOS 26 or later. Pro's minutes need Apple Intelligence.

Is anything recorded?

Only if you choose to keep conversations. They stay on your phone and in your own iCloud, and the other person sees that it's being saved.

How accurate is it?

It's machine translation, so it can make mistakes. When it can't confirm a line, it asks for it again instead of showing a guess. For medical or legal conversations, use a professional interpreter.
